# KaliArc AI Lab branding

## Product name

**KaliArc AI Lab**

## Tagline

Local-first AI command centre for Kali Linux.

## One-liner

KaliArc AI Lab builds a private local AI workstation on Kali with Ollama, Open WebUI, MCP agents, scoped Kali tool integrations, start-menu launchers, and optional HexStrike.

## Design idea

KaliArc is built around the idea of an **arc** between two modes:

1. General local AI work: writing, coding, sysadmin, study, automation, and troubleshooting.
2. Authorised security lab workflows: scoped analysis wrappers for Kali tools and optional HexStrike.

The logo uses:

- A hexagonal lab/control shape.
- A glowing arc for local AI orchestration.
- A terminal prompt mark for Linux-first workflows.
- Neural nodes for agents and MCP connectivity.
